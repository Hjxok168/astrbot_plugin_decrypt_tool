## Reporting a Vulnerability 

We take security issues seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and will make every effort to acknowledge, verify, and address vulnerabilities promptly. 

### How to Report a Vulnerability

To report a security vulnerability, please email us at [hxqok@outlook.com](mailto:security@example.com). Please include the following information:

- Description of the vulnerability
- Steps to reproduce the vulnerability
- Potential impact of the vulnerability
- Any suggested mitigation strategies

### What to Expect

- **Acknowledgment**: We will acknowledge receipt of your vulnerability report within 48 hours.
- **Update Timeline**: We will provide updates on the status of the vulnerability at least once every 7 days.
- **Validation Process**: Our security team will validate the vulnerability and determine its severity.

### Vulnerability Response

- **Accepted Vulnerabilities**: If the vulnerability is accepted, we will work to patch it and release an update as soon as possible. We will also acknowledge your contribution in the release notes if you wish.
- **Declined Vulnerabilities**: If we determine that the reported issue is not a vulnerability or does not meet our criteria, we will explain our reasoning in detail.

### Disclosure Policy

- We prefer to handle security issues privately and work with researchers to resolve them before public disclosure.
- We aim to resolve critical vulnerabilities within 30 days.
- After a vulnerability is resolved, you may disclose it publicly after giving us 7 days to deploy the fix.

### Recognition

We appreciate the efforts of security researchers and may publicly acknowledge your contribution (with your permission) in our security advisory or release notes.
